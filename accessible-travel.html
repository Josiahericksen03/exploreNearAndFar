Accessible travel

Mobility, sensory, and medical needs from the first call — not a checkbox after the booking. Faith is an SNG Certified Accessible Travel Advocate.

We ask the questions up front: wheelchair dimensions, transfer needs, hearing or vision support, service animals, and what “accessible room” actually means at a given resort or ship.

  • Verified accessible cabins on cruises and stateroom locations that work
  • Ground transfers that can accommodate scooters or wheelchairs
  • Resorts with step-free routes and appropriate bathroom layouts
  • Advocacy if a supplier’s “accessible” option does not match reality
