Accessible travel

Accessible Travel Advocate

Accessible travel isn’t a checkbox — it’s asking the right questions up front: wheelchair dimensions, transfer needs, hearing or vision support, service animals, and what “accessible room” actually means at a given resort or ship.

We research so you don’t have to guess

  • Verified accessible cabins on cruises and accessible stateroom locations
  • Ground transfers that can accommodate scooters or wheelchairs
  • Resorts with step-free routes and appropriate bathroom layouts
  • Advocacy if a supplier’s “accessible” option doesn’t match reality

This site mockup is built with WCAG 2.1 AA in mind — readable type, strong contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation, and labeled forms — because your travelers notice when the details are done right.
