We verify “accessible” before you pay for it

Wheelchair dimensions, transfer needs, hearing or vision support, service animals — and what an “accessible room” actually means at that resort or on that ship.

The word means different things to different suppliers. We ask the specific questions, get the answers in writing where we can, and tell you plainly when an option does not meet the standard you need.

  • Verified accessible cabins and stateroom locations on cruise ships
  • Ground transfers that can take a scooter or a wheelchair
  • Resorts with step-free routes and bathroom layouts that work
  • Advocacy if a supplier’s “accessible” option does not match reality on arrival

A certified advocate, and a former nurse

Faith trained as a nurse before she sold a single trip. The SNG Accessible Travel Advocate certification adds the supplier-side knowledge: which lines, ships, and resorts deliver, and how to hold them to it.

This site is built to WCAG 2.1 AA with the same care — readable type, strong contrast, keyboard navigation, labeled forms. Read the accessibility statement.
