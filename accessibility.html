Accessibility statement

We want every visitor — including seniors and travelers using assistive technology — to use this site with ease.

Explore Near & Far Travel, LLC is committed to providing a website that conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA where reasonably possible. This mockup demonstrates intended practices: readable default text size, strong color contrast, descriptive image alt text, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, and clearly labeled forms.

Feedback

If anything on this site is difficult to use, please contact us through the Plan Your Trip form and note “Accessibility” in your message. We will work with you to provide the information in an alternative format.

Ongoing effort

New photos, videos, and pages will include text descriptions and captions before publication, per our content guidelines.
