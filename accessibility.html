Accessibility statement

We want every visitor — including seniors and travelers using assistive technology — to use this site with ease.

Explore Near & Far Travel, LLC is committed to a website that conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA where reasonably possible. This mockup demonstrates the intended practices: readable default text size, strong color contrast, descriptive image alt text, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, labeled forms, and reduced-motion alternatives for video and animation.

Feedback

If anything on this site is difficult to use, call 407-508-0990 or use the Plan Your Trip form and note “Accessibility” in your message. We will provide the information in an alternative format.

Ongoing effort

New photos, videos, and pages will include text descriptions before publication.
