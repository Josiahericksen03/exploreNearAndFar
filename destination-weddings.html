From “Yes” to “I Do” and Beyond

Custom Travel Planning. Real Value. Personal Service.

Your love story deserves more than a single day. From the moment you say yes to every anniversary that follows, we want to be your go-to for every trip along the way. We’ll handle the details so you can enjoy the moments that matter.

Wedding planning is personal to us. Faith owned a bed-and-breakfast inn where she hosted more than 300 weddings. She knows how many moving pieces come together for one beautiful day, and she knows how much it means when someone else keeps track of them for you.

The Celebration Starts

  • Engagement and proposal trips: a romantic getaway to pop the question or celebrate the yes
  • Bachelor and bachelorette getaways: beach weekends, cruises, city escapes and group trips with friends

The Big Day

  • Destination weddings: resorts, cruises and venues near and far, with travel plans for you and your guests
  • Guest accommodations: hotel room blocks and travel help for out-of-town guests, even if the wedding is in your own backyard
  • Group coordination: one point of contact for your guests’ questions, bookings and travel details, so you don’t have to be the travel desk

And Beyond

  • Honeymoons: all-inclusive resorts, cruises, European getaways and once-in-a-lifetime adventures
  • Babymoons: a relaxing escape before your family grows
  • Anniversaries and vow renewals: milestone trips to celebrate the years together
  • Private villa celebrations: an Italian villa with a private chef for a vow renewal, milestone anniversary or wedding weekend with your closest family and friends
