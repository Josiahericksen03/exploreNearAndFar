Land Vacations Planned Around You

Custom Travel Planning. Real Value. Personal Service.

From Caribbean beaches to Walt Disney World to the streets of Florence, we plan land vacations around your interests, pace and budget. We believe ongoing education and personal research are essential to good advice, so we keep learning through supplier training, certifications and our own travels.

Sandals and Beaches Specialists

We’ve completed Sandals Elite Advisor training to help you choose the right resort and room.

  • Sandals: adults-only all-inclusive resorts for couples, honeymoons and anniversaries
  • Beaches: all-inclusive family resorts and the first resort company in the world to be designated an Advanced Certified Autism Center. Beach wheelchairs are available, and we’ll request one in advance.

Disney Vacation Specialists

We’re certified Disney specialists based in Celebration, just minutes from Walt Disney World. We’ll help you plan parks, resorts, dining and tickets, and we can pair your stay with a Disney cruise from Port Canaveral.

Europe and Worldwide Travel

Italy has our hearts, from Florence and Venice to Rome and Cinque Terre. We’ve also explored France, England, Scotland, Iceland, the Netherlands, Belgium, Poland, Prague, Estonia, Istanbul, Casablanca and Mexico. We plan custom itineraries, escorted tours, all-inclusive resorts, and private villas in Italy with a private chef for special celebrations.

Don’t see your dream destination here? We plan trips all over the world, including places still on our own wish list. Just because it isn’t listed doesn’t mean we can’t plan it. Through careful research and trusted suppliers, we’ll help you get there.
