Senior Travel: Your Time, Your Way

Retirement is a great time to see the world. We plan trips that fit how you want to travel now, with an easier pace, fewer hotel changes and someone else taking care of the details.

Create Your Bucket List

You meet with your accountant every year to plan ahead. Your travel deserves the same attention. Let’s meet once a year to talk about the places you dream of, the people you want to see and the celebrations coming up, like an anniversary, the grandkids’ first cruise or a visit with family and friends you’ve missed. Travel keeps your mind active, keeps you connected and gives you something to look forward to all year. Planning early also gives you more choices and more value for your money.

Why Travel With Us

We build each trip around your interests, energy and budget. We travel often ourselves, so we know what makes a trip relaxing. If anything changes, you can call a real person who knows your trip and will step in to help.

Trips That Work Well

We suggest river cruises, ocean cruises, escorted tours and all-inclusive resorts. We also help solo travelers find small groups and trips with lower single supplements.
