# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS mockup (existing codebase)

## Users

Travelers planning a trip who want a person, not a call center — especially couples arranging wedding travel, seniors who want a slower pace, and people with mobility, sensory, or medical needs. Family and group travelers booking cruises or land vacations are a second audience. Primary scene: someone in Central Florida (or calling Orlando) comparing a cruise, resort, or wedding block and needing someone who will still pick up if a flight changes.

## Product Purpose

Explore Near & Far Travel, LLC is Faith and Rob Ericksen, personal travel advisors in Orlando. The site should make it obvious what they plan, that you talk to them directly, and how to start a trip inquiry. Success for this mockup: a professional, accessible brochure that can move to WordPress later.

## Positioning

You talk to Faith or Rob from the first idea through disruptions. There is no fee to book through them; travel partners compensate them. Faith is a Virgin Voyages Certified First Mate and Commodore partner, a former innkeeper who hosted 300+ weddings, and an SNG Certified Accessible Travel Advocate.
