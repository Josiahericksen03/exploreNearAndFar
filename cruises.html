Cruises

Are you dreaming of Caribbean sunsets, Alaska glaciers at dawn, or a café view of a cobblestone square? We match you with the cruise line and ship that fit your budget — family fun, adventure, or luxury dining and entertainment.

Cruise pricing is rarely “what you see is what you get.” Promotions stack, onboard credits change, and cabin location matters. Faith is a Virgin Voyages Certified First Mate and Commodore partner who can walk you through adult-only sailing, dining, and what to book before you board.

Viking ocean, river & expedition

From European rivers and scenic fjords to expedition sailing in Antarctica, Viking’s smaller ships and destination-focused itineraries appeal to travelers who want culture, comfort, and time ashore. As Viking Certified Experts, we help you compare seasons, cabin categories, and what’s included — so the ship matches how you like to travel.

  • River cruises with walk-off-the-ship access to towns and cities
  • Ocean voyages with understated luxury and strong dining
  • Expedition-style trips for wildlife and remote coastlines
