Cruises

Caribbean sunsets, Alaska glaciers, or a café on a cobblestone square — we match you with the line and ship that fit the trip, not the brochure.

Cruise pricing is rarely “what you see is what you get.” Promotions stack, onboard credits change, and cabin location matters. Faith is a Virgin Voyages Certified First Mate and Commodore partner who can walk you through adult-only sailing, dining, and what to book before you board.

We help with sailings from Florida ports convenient to Central Florida, RockStar suites and accessible staterooms when needed, and group travel for milestones or reunions.

Cruise Vacations Planned Around You

Custom Travel Planning. Real Value. Personal Service.

Explore Near and Far Travel is a family-owned agency in Celebration, Florida, minutes from Walt Disney World and near Port Canaveral. We match you with the cruise line, ship and itinerary that fit how you travel, and we stay with you from the first question until you’re home.

Certified Cruise Specialists

We’ve completed specialist training with Disney Cruise Line, Viking, Royal Caribbean, Cunard, Virgin Voyages, Princess Cruises and Carnival Cruise Line, and we also plan sailings with other major lines. We book ocean, river and expedition cruises to the Caribbean, Alaska, Europe and beyond.

Disney Cruises and Disney Vacations

We’re certified in both Disney Cruise Line and Disney land vacations. We can plan a Disney cruise from Port Canaveral, a Walt Disney World stay, or both together in one easy trip, with resort, dining and park recommendations that fit your family.

Pre- and Post-Cruise Land Extensions

Make the most of your trip by adding time on land before or after your cruise. We arrange hotels, transfers, tours and multi-day land packages. Pair a Disney cruise with a few days at Walt Disney World, explore Rome before a Mediterranean sailing, or see the Canadian Rockies after an Alaska cruise. Arriving a day or two early also takes the stress out of flight delays.

Cruises for Seniors

Cruising is one of the easiest ways for seniors to see the world: you unpack once, meals are included, and there’s a new destination almost every day. We recommend ships and itineraries with the right pace, comfortable cabins, port days that aren’t too strenuous, and enrichment, dining and entertainment you’ll enjoy.

Accessible Cruise Planning

We’re certified accessible travel advocates through Special Needs Group. We help travelers with mobility, hearing, vision or medical needs find the right ship and cabin, arrange equipment such as wheelchairs, scooters or oxygen, and advocate with the cruise line on your behalf.
